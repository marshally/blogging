# The Two Scoreboards: Fan-Love vs. Peer-Admiration

Author's original layer (not from the literature): two legibility systems that disagree,
and disagree most for exactly the Implementer and Teamworker roles.

- **Scoreboard 1 (fans / users / promotion committees):** what's visible from the front
  of the stage — charisma, solos, spectacle, attributable moments.
- **Scoreboard 2 (practitioners):** craft legible only from inside the discipline.

Theory tie-back: Bales predicts the gap. Task-pole work produces attributable artifacts;
social-emotional and infrastructural work produces *properties of the whole* (blend,
cohesion, reliability) that can't be attributed to anyone until removed. Fan-love tracks
attributable output; peer-admiration tracks whole-system properties.

Software mapping: the promotion-committee problem; Reilly's "Being Glue"; the Alex
problem (infra engineer legible only to infra engineers) and the Anthony problem
(admired by peers *for glue itself*, which review systems can't see).

## Evidence (web research, 2026-07-11)

### Alex (drummers' drummer, invisible to fan lists)
- Wikipedia consensus framing: "noted for his technical prowess, speed, and power...
  widely regarded as one of the greatest drummers of all time."
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Van_Halen
- Drummer-press cataloguing: Modern Drummer feature (Wurster, June 2012, "Of Sound and
  Vision"); DRUM! Magazine tributes. https://drummagazine.com/van-halen-drummer/
- Craft-detail exhibit (USED IN POST): the swinging double-kick + ride-bell work at the
  end of "Girl Gone Bad" — cited in drummer-forum discussion as something you must
  "listen real hard" to catch; same threads note his absence from 100-best lists and
  attribute it to Eddie's shadow + frontman drama.
  https://www.drumchat.com/showthread.php/26947-Alex-Van-Halen-Underrated
- Peer testimony: Dale Crover (Melvins) naming Alex among the most underrated drummers,
  overshadowed by band leads. https://rockcelebrities.net/the-drummer-alex-van-halen-considered-the-most-underrated/
- Recursion (CUT at 3.5k, kept for reference): Alex made the same argument about John
  Bonham — praised his "dynamics and sensitivity," was disgusted Bonham got half a page
  in Circus Magazine at his death. The underrated Implementer advocating for underrated
  Implementers. Same source as above.
- CAUTION (D5): fan-side evidence is forum sentiment + listicle absence — softer. Frame
  as "drummer publications and fellow musicians call him underrated," not direct claims
  about fan opinion.

### Anthony (the secret weapon; admired for glue, not bass)
- Independent convergence on the epithet across outlets:
  - "Michael Anthony Sobolewski was Van Halen's secret weapon — so much so that when he
    was 'excused' from the band, samples of his backing vocals were piped in for their
    live shows..." https://knowyourbassplayer.com/2020/05/19/2020-5-19-michael-anthony/
  - "...the bassist's backing vocal prowess earned him the reputation of the band's
    secret weapon in the classic David Lee Roth VH era."
    https://www.musicradar.com/news/van-halen-michael-anthony-aint-talkin-bout-love
  - "While Anthony is as reliable a rock bassist as there ever was, it's the brightness
    and precision of his backing vocals that made him such a secret weapon... preferred
    playing a supporting role throughout his career."
    https://www.iheart.com/content/2022-02-17-watch-michael-anthony-sing-lead-vocals-on-two-roth-era-van-halen-hits/
- Bass framing: "reliable," "rock-solid" — respected, never virtuosic. Praise is always
  the harmonies, never the bass lines.
- Album-level natural experiment (CUT at 3.5k — liner notes carry the claim instead):
  critics tie the vocal thinness of *Balance* / *Van Halen III* to Anthony's reduced
  presence; "glorified cover band" framing.
  https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/michael-anthony-the-real-secret-weapon-of-van-halen/
- THE KICKER FACT (non-negotiable in any version): piped-in vocal samples post-firing.
  Keep "reportedly" — secondary-sourced.
