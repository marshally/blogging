# Hagar as Resource Investigator: corroboration for the recasting

Research run 2026-07-13, prompted by Marshall's discomfort with the unsubstantiated "Coordinator-adjacent" casting (see D17; frameworks.md never assigned Hagar a role, and timeline-evidence.md gives the Coordinator function to Leffler). One agent, three buckets: corroborate RI during VH tenure, corroborate the durable post-VH pattern, and adversarially test both the old casting and the RI weaknesses.

## Verdict

The evidence supports recasting Hagar as **Resource Investigator** — the same Belbin type as Roth, which sharpens the Act II thesis: the frontman seat attracted the same archetype twice; what changed was when he arrived (and note the two RIs never shared a lineup, so this doesn't trip Belbin's duplicated-roles failure mode). The strongest corroboration is the pairing of a hard business fact (the $80M Campari deal) with a contemporaneous in-band conflict fact (the 2004 reunion contract clause banning Cabo Wabo shirts onstage). The strongest caveat: the classic RI weakness ("loses interest once enthusiasm passes") is only half-true for Hagar — fits the restaurant closures, contradicted by a decade-plus of hands-on tequila brand work — so the post should cast the type without asserting the full stereotype. No evidence anywhere for Hagar as in-band chairperson/mediator; the only "peacemaking" on record is post-breakup and one-directional, which argues against the old casting.

## Bucket 1: RI behaviors during VH tenure (1985–1996, plus 2004 reunion)

**Cabo Wabo Cantina, opened April 1990, mid-tenure**, initially with Eddie, Alex, and Anthony as partners. It lost money for three years; the "always-optimistic" Hagar bought the others out. VinePair (third-party retelling, ultimately traceable to Hagar's account): https://vinepair.com/articles/sammy-hagar-cabo-wabo-tequila/ — corroborated by https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabo_Wabo. Hagar's own self-assessment in the same piece: "I'm not a good businessman at all, I'm crazy, I'm an idiot."

**Cabo Wabo Tequila, launched 1996** as the cantina's house brand (national distribution professionalized 1999 — cite "1996" as brand creation, not rollout). Grew from 37,000 cases year one to 140,000 by 2006, the #2 premium tequila in the US.

**In-band friction over the hustle.** Hagar to Rolling Stone, 2020 (self-sourced, verified): "I'm a business guy. I love business. I love creating things, and seeing them make it... My ambition and my creativity just drove them nuts because they didn't understand it." https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/sammy-hagar-van-halen-reunion-eddie-van-halen-1234600146/

**The 2004 shirt ban — the kicker fact.** The reunion contract barred Hagar from wearing Cabo Wabo shirts or mentioning the tequila/cantina onstage; he tattooed the logo on his shoulder so the jumbotron would carry it anyway. Ultimate Classic Rock (verified): https://ultimateclassicrock.com/sammy-hagar-van-halen-cabo-wabo-shirts-ban/ — Hagar's telling, but the contractual clause is corroborated across outlets.

**The 2007 Campari sale.** 80% of Cabo Wabo Tequila to Gruppo Campari for **$80 million**, announced May 7, 2007; Hagar kept 20% and stayed brand ambassador. Verified across UCR (https://ultimateclassicrock.com/sammy-hagar-cabo-wabo-tequila/), Wikipedia, and Forbes 2017 (https://www.forbes.com/sites/zackomalleygreenburg/2017/03/23/flying-high-with-the-red-rocker-sammy-hagar-talks-jets-booze-and-buffett/). The RI-flavored quote on the deal's logic: "To get my tequila everywhere in the world, I had to go with the big liquor companies. When you walk into a bar in Hong Kong, you want to see Cabo Wabo behind the bar."

**Motive self-description.** "I've never started a business thinking, Oh, I'm gonna make money off of this. All my ideas have come from sheer enthusiasm." — Inc. Magazine, 2013 (https://www.inc.com/magazine/201311/liz-welch/sammy-hagar.html). CAUTION: page 403'd on direct fetch; confirmed only via search snippet. Echoed in the verified Forbes 2017 piece: "I never thought any of it would really make money. It was just passion."

## Bucket 2: post-VH durable pattern (brief — establishes the trait held)

Chickenfoot (2008): Hagar personally recruited Satriani and Chad Smith — Smith's own words: "Sammy invited me to play at the club on New Year's Eve" (Louder Sound, verified: https://www.loudersound.com/features/chickenfoot-sammy-hagar-interview). The Circle (2014–). Sammy's Beach Bar Rum, Santo Tequila (partner reported as Fieri and/or Levine — sourcing conflict, check before citing), Red Rocker Brewing, restaurant chain, AXS TV's *Rock & Roll Road Trip*, syndicated radio. The Buffett jet anecdote (Forbes 2017, self-sourced).

## Bucket 3: adversarial findings

**Against the old Coordinator casting:** targeted searches (mediator, peacemaker, voice of reason, 5150-era band-dynamics accounts) found nothing showing Hagar as in-band goal-clarifier or decision-broker. The reconciliation narrative (peace with Eddie before his death, wanting peace with Alex) is post-breakup, decades later, one-directional.

**RI weakness is inconsistent — hedge required:** fits the restaurants (El Paseo closed 2018, Hagar: "After 7 years, running the restaurant became too time consuming" — ABC7, verified: https://abc7news.com/sammy-hagar-el-paseo-restaurant-mill-valley/3756394/; the Roseville licensee failure — CBS Sacramento, verified). Contradicted by the tequila: a decade-plus of personal agave-grower relationships and QC in Jalisco (Cigar Aficionado, verified: https://www.cigaraficionado.com/article/talkin-tequila-with-sammy-hagar-3739), and he stayed on through the brand's 2009 sales decline rather than walking. Cast the type, not the stereotype.
