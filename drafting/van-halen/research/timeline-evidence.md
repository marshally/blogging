# Timeline Evidence: Episodes, Framework Readings, Software Parallels

Sourcing rule (DECISIONS.md D4): observable record only — interviews, memoirs, liner
notes, credits, public letters. No inner-motive assertions. "Reportedly" for
secondary-sourced claims. Where accounts conflict, the conflict is the data.

## Act I: Two Shapers, One Seat (1974–1985)

### Templeman as external Coordinator siding with Roth
Producer Ted Templeman functioned as external Coordinator/Monitor Evaluator and tended
to side with Roth on creative direction (short, poppy, guitar-forward, no synths).
Sources: Templeman's memoir *A Platinum Producer's Life in Music* (2020); Renoff,
*Van Halen Rising* (2015). Software parallel: the principal architect / board member
whose alignment silently decides which internal faction wins design reviews.

### The 5150 Studios build (1983)
Eddie built 5150 at his home (with engineer Donn Landee), described across interviews as
an explicit move to control recording. Framework: the blocked Plant exits shared
infrastructure. Software parallel: the architect who, tired of losing reviews, builds it
on a side branch until it's a fait accompli.

### "Jump" — the decisive artifact
Synth riff rejected for years (Eddie in multiple interviews; Templeman's account
corroborates resistance); recorded at 5150; the band's only #1 single. STRUCTURAL
CENTERPIECE: pays off the cold-open soundtrack anecdote. The Plant wins outside the
sanctioned process.

### The 1985 split
Contest decided → task conflict migrates to relationship conflict (Jehn) → Roth exits
within a year of the commercial peak. Roth's solo career = documented evidence of a
Shaper claimant's self-conception (no motive-reading needed; the career itself is the record).

## Act II: The Settled Seat (1985–1996)

### Sequence, not temperament
Hagar joined AFTER the structural contest was decided — Eddie's band, Eddie's terms,
Eddie's studio. Hagar is not temperamentally mild (*Red*, 2011, passim). Software
parallel: co-founder direction wars vs. decorated senior hire post-architecture.

### Commercial record
Four consecutive #1 albums (5150, OU812, For Unlawful Carnal Knowledge, Balance) —
more commercial success than the Roth era; contested iconic status. Harmony-vs-creative-
tension question; Jehn again.

### Ed Leffler (d. 1993) → Ray Danniels
Hagar's *Red* credits manager Leffler as the neutral stabilizer (the in-house Coordinator
the band never otherwise had). Replacement Ray Danniels: Alex's former brother-in-law,
in Hagar's telling aligned with the brothers. Chairperson seat refilled by a partisan.
Software parallel: beloved neutral EM replaced by someone from one side of a standing rivalry.

### The exit (1996)
Proximate disputes: *Twister* soundtrack songs ("Humans Being"), greatest-hits album
Hagar opposed. Accounts conflict (*Red* vs. brothers' contemporaneous interviews) —
presented as data: with no Coordinator, even the story of the conflict couldn't be agreed.

## Act III: Role Collapse and Succession (1996–2020)

### 1996 MTV VMA reunion
Roth rejoined believing full reunion; VMA appearance Sept 1996; collapse within weeks;
Roth's open letter (Oct 1996, widely reprinted) vs. Eddie's interview accounts. Claim:
structural problems don't age out — 11 years dormant, reignited in days. Software
parallel: the boomerang co-founder experiment.

### Cherone (1998–99)
Hired from Extreme; one album; gone in ~two years. A role is a negotiated relationship
with specific people, not a seat staffed from a resume. By 1998 the frontman slot was so
subordinate it provided no counterweight. Software parallel: the impressive mis-hire.

Primary-source refinement: Cherone later described *Van Halen III* as a studio production made while he was still getting to know the others; only after touring did they become a band. In an earlier retrospective he called the album "really Eddie's baby" and said it needed more guidance.

Framework reading: the band staffed frontman as though it were a **Specialist** slot — find a proven singer and insert him into the org chart — even though frontman was a negotiated relationship and potential counterweight. This is an interpretation of the structure, not a claim about anyone's motives, and it does not assign Cherone the **Specialist** role.

### Van Halen III (1998) — role absorption in the liner notes
Verifiable credits: Eddie played bass on most tracks (Anthony on only three); Eddie sang
lead on "How Many Say I"; co-produced with Mike Post, heavy Eddie involvement. One
member occupying Plant + Shaper + Completer Finisher + pieces of Teamworker/frontman;
no Monitor Evaluator to kill it in review. The credits ARE the argument — zero
motive-reading required. Pays off the ME-vacancy foreshadowing.

### Anthony's demotion and exit (1996–2006)
Songwriting/royalty position reduced late 90s; reportedly accepted diminished terms to
join the 2004 reunion tour (contemporary press; Anthony in later interviews, e.g. Eddie
Trunk); replaced by Wolfgang in 2006, by several accounts learning via press.
THE PUNCHLINE: recordings of his backing vocals reportedly piped into subsequent live
shows — the org removed the glue person and built tooling to fake his output.
Software parallels: glue-person layoff + replacement tooling; Reilly's "Being Glue."
Completes the Act I plant (inward-facing social-emotional pole, lost).

### Succession and armistice (2006–2020) — COMPRESSED PER D6
Wolfgang: the ultimate new guy, structurally incapable of contesting the Shaper seat;
also genuinely excellent. 2012–15 Roth era: *A Different Kind of Truth*, postponed 2012
tour dates, tense armistice — seat contest rendered moot by total structural victory
(his studio, his son, his name). THE ONE DIRECT QUOTE: Eddie, Billboard 2015:
"he does not want to be my friend." Eddie died October 6, 2020. Few restrained
sentences, no elegy.

## Constant negative space
Alex: only member besides Eddie in every lineup; zero self-generated public conflicts in
40+ years. The Implementer thesis supported by his absence from the conflict record.
