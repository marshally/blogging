**Pattern:** corroboration dies, exhibits survive; methodological honesty dies almost
completely; nothing structural dies. The 3k asserts the lens; the 6k defends it.

## The seven restorations → 3,500-word ship target

Priority-ordered; restore causal connective tissue before corroboration or ornament.

1. **Billboard quote** (~15w). The piece's only human voice; closes the Roth arc in nine words.
2. **Woolley/Aristotle complication** (~120w). Cheapest credibility purchase available.
3. **Templeman as external Coordinator siding with Roth** (~80w, Act I). Without it,
   "Eddie built a studio" is a fact; with it, it's a move in the war. Highest-value pure
   restoration — fills a causal hole.
4. **Two Act III software parallels** (~80w): glue-layoff-then-tooling (at the samples
   beat) + impressive mis-hire (at Cherone). Serves the actual audience.
5. **Anthony as inward social pole, planted in Act I** (~40w). Converts Act III's best
   moment from assertion into payoff.
6. **Accounts-differ → one honest methodological sentence** (~50w, at Twister). Honesty
   AND argument: nobody agreeing is evidence of life without a Coordinator.
7. **Monitor Evaluator gap foreshadowed → VH III payoff** (~40w, cast sheet). Makes VH III
   feel inevitable rather than itemized.

Remaining ~75w of slack: spend on the fog (let the ending breathe to 200–225w), not content.

**Stays dead even at 3,500:** Bonham recursion, Crover + magazine corroboration,
Balance/VH III critics, Benne & Sheats, Roth open-letter detail, Wolfgang pedigree,
physics/chemistry frame. All corroboration or ornament; nothing causal on the floor.

**Finding:** 3,500 is the piece's natural floor — below it cuts tendons, above it adds
muscle the post doesn't need.
