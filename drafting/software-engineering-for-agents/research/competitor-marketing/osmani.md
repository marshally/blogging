# Addy Osmani: social media and content marketing

Research date: **2026-10-13**. Priority window: **2026-07-16 through 2026-10-13**, supplemented by the *Beyond Vibe Coding* launch and earlier examples that establish reuse. This is a public-evidence marketing analysis, not a review of the books or an audit of business results. Recommendations are proposals for *Standing on the Shoulders of Giants: A Software Engineering Primer for Agentic Coding*.

## Decisive findings

**Osmani distributes substantial free instruction through several connected surfaces, then offers books and continued readership.** The [book microsite](https://beyond.addy.ie/) teaches through diagrams, practical examples, and checklists before asking visitors to buy the book. His [Practical Loop Engineering article](https://addyosmani.com/blog/practical-loop-engineering/) credits a Substack original and currently ends with a next-book link and a free-newsletter invitation. The observable commercial paths are books, publisher membership, and newsletter sponsorship. A consulting intake or paid workshop funnel was not established.

**There is direct evidence of reuse, but the proven sequences differ from articles becoming batches of short videos.** A [75-second LinkedIn video](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/addyosmani_ai-programming-softwareengineering-activity-7451880794958008320-k6uY) identifies its source as a JSNation discussion and links the full recording. [Own the Outer Loop](https://addyosmani.com/blog/own-the-outer-loop/) identifies itself as the written form of a conference keynote. A [React article](https://addyo.substack.com/p/how-good-is-ai-at-coding-react-really) also identifies a keynote as its source. These establish long conversation → clip and talk → article. They do not establish a repeatable article → 2–10 clips process.

**The free product is itself an authority asset.** [Agent Skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) makes the teaching operational through installable workflows, checklists, and examples. Its [August 19 LinkedIn promotion](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/addyosmani_agent-skills-hit-88k-stars-we-also-just-activity-7495728872920023041-NDjA) combines a popularity milestone, new tutorials, release details, and an invitation for feedback. The tutorial and release supply a new reason to discuss the same underlying engineering thesis.

**This is an established educator's distribution network.** Osmani's [homepage](https://addyosmani.com/) connects books, open source, social accounts, articles, talks, and appearances. Conference organizers, The Pragmatic Engineer, Google for Developers, and O'Reilly supply separate publishing surfaces. Their audiences and production resources should not be attributed to his personal accounts. The transferable practice is making one useful body of teaching available in several appropriate forms. Reproducing his reach is a different proposition.

## What is actually being repurposed?

**Confirmed: long interview → native short video.** The April LinkedIn example supplies the source attribution, full-recording link, and video duration in public metadata. The full recording's YouTube oEmbed identifies JavaScript Conferences by GitNation as the channel. The post teaches a complete argument about review and architectural context, then offers the longer conversation. This is the clearest inspected example relevant to Marshall's short-video plan.

**Confirmed: conference talk → illustrated article.** The July 15 [Outer Loop article](https://addyosmani.com/blog/own-the-outer-loop/) explicitly identifies the AI Engineer World's Fair keynote as its source and embeds the recording. Its sections contain teaching slides. The December React piece independently confirms the same reuse practice for a GitNation keynote. Images create reusable explanation units, although their presence does not prove LinkedIn carousel publication.

**Confirmed: newsletter → blog → social adaptation.** The Practical Loop Engineering blog credits Substack as its original location. Both display August 14, and the LinkedIn explanation is dated August 15 UTC. The social post teaches the distinction between goals and loops and links the full article. Current versions have different commercial surroundings: sponsor messages on Substack, next-book and newsletter invitations on the blog. The observations establish a connected distribution path, not which site acquired more readers.

**Confirmed: personal blog → publisher republication.** The July 20 Software Factories piece reappears on O'Reilly on September 18 with an explicit permission and origin notice. This gives one substantial article another publication occasion. The publisher owns that distribution surface, and editorial access is a prerequisite.

**Not established: a systematic article → multiple Shorts assembly line.** A personal site displaying articles and Shorts is insufficient. The inspected effectiveness Short is hosted by Google for Developers, and the book interview is hosted by The Pragmatic Engineer. Channel coexistence, related topics, and a curated homepage do not reveal script origins, editing labor, or the number of clips generated per source.

## Conversion paths and commercial boundaries

The following paths were inspected without submitting forms, subscribing, or purchasing:

1. **Social book milestone → free companion site → retailer.** The September final-draft post links the companion sites. Beyond Vibe Coding supplies a substantial free guide and a **“Buy the Book”** link to Amazon. The purchase CTA is visible without requiring email capture. The microsite's educational depth is an observable practice, not evidence that its advice increases sales.
2. **Free article → continued readership or next book.** The current Practical Loop Engineering blog footer points to *Agentic Engineering* on O'Reilly and invites a free newsletter subscription. This connects new writing to a continuing author catalog. Do not assume that today's footer was present on the original publication date.
3. **Newsletter issue → sponsor destination.** The Substack version discloses Trigger.dev sponsorship near the opening and again near the end. Sponsor presence establishes commercial inventory. It does not reveal the fee, attributable customers, or Osmani's earnings.
4. **Publisher discovery → publisher membership or book purchase.** O'Reilly's catalog offers full access and buying options. Its Superstream recording similarly promotes membership. Those are publisher commercial paths, not evidence that Osmani operates a personal course business.
5. **Free software → usage and feedback.** Agent Skills provides installation and adoption instructions. The LinkedIn tutorial promotion asks for feedback. No sales attribution or downstream consulting pipeline was established from that path.

There is no verified consulting offer, consultation booking page, personal workshop price, lead qualification form, or book-to-consulting conversion result in this inspected set. This is an evidence boundary. It does not establish that Osmani never consults or teaches privately.

## Proposals for the consulting-led primer

These tests retain the accepted articles → 2–10 short videos → talks → book → workshops → course sequence. They do not add a posting quota or replace consulting with a creator-product business.

| Practice worth testing | Small test | Prerequisite and useful measure |
|---|---|---|
| Make a free lesson useful before asking for anything | Publish one complete classics-based lesson with a failure example, named source, agent instruction, and observable check. Put an optional team-help invitation beside the archive/subscription paths. | One demonstrated case and a defined consulting problem. Measure relevant replies and qualified inquiries separately from visits. The consulting CTA is Marshall's proposed adaptation, not a copied Osmani funnel. |
| Design explanation units that survive format changes | For one article, record two short lessons, each completing one argument. Reuse the same tested example and one legible diagram. | Recording/editing time and readable code capture. Compare substantive questions and viewing behavior across the two clips. Do not assume a reach boost or copy Google's production budget. |
| Provide a small usable artifact | Release one checklist or agent instruction that implements a named classic's principle, linked to its demonstration and limitations. | A public repository, maintenance owner, and a bounded support commitment. Track reported use and failure cases. Avoid building a full skill marketplace to market the first article. |
| Reuse a successful explanation with attribution | Turn one talk or recorded workshop segment into an article revision and a clip, retaining a source map between versions. | Permission to reuse the recording and evidence the explanation landed. Track the new format's questions and invitations, not a fabricated cross-channel conversion total. |
| Give readers a durable destination | Create a primer index with complete sample lessons, source lineage, subscription, and a clear path to discuss a team problem. | Existing owned-site and email infrastructure. Test whether a relevant reader understands both the teaching promise and when to contact Marshall. No book checkout is needed yet. |

Osmani's combination of practical free writing and usable software is especially relevant to a curriculum that turns classics into agent behavior. Marshall's stronger proposed distinction remains explicit intellectual lineage, a coherent sequence, and a checkable demonstration. Competing on output volume would require capacity this research has not measured.

## Evidence limits and do-not-use ledger

- **Verified primary:** author pages and posts establish public practices; platform structured metadata establishes the recorded dates and counts; publisher and host pages establish their own promotion, access paths, and publication dates. All were publicly accessible at inspection except the X feed.
- **Editorial inference:** audience descriptions, the interpretation of these connected surfaces as a marketing approach, and all proposed adaptations. Commercial intent cannot be assigned to every individual educational post.
- **Format limits:** illustrated articles and native video are established. A regular LinkedIn document-carousel program is not. Homepage-linked Instagram and TikTok accounts were not audited as feeds. No unsupported channel absence is asserted.
- **Date limits:** LinkedIn relative labels varied across crawls. Use the recorded JSON-LD dates, not “one month ago.” Original date, current footer state, retrieval date, video upload date, and publisher republication date are different facts.
- **Attribution limits:** the Google-hosted Short, GitNation interview, AI Engineer keynote, and Pragmatic Engineer podcast are externally hosted. O'Reilly's audience and membership business belong to the publisher. Employer affiliation supplies biographical context, not a measured personal acquisition channel.
- **Do not use:** 600,000 as an email-list size; followers as unique people; stars as active users; reactions as conversions; the 70% framing as a measured marketing result; an assumption that every blog post produces multiple videos; an asserted daily cadence; an unverified personal consulting/course business; or any guaranteed LinkedIn distribution advantage.

Research method: public web search and direct first-party page reads; public LinkedIn HTML JSON-LD for exact dates, format, duration, and counts; YouTube oEmbed for the named recording/channel checks; and unauthenticated Bluesky public profile/feed reads. No outreach, follows, subscriptions, purchases, or account changes were made.
