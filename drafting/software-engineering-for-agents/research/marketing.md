# Marketing research and planning inputs

Checked 2026-10-12. Supports the [marketing plan](../marketing-plan.md).

## M01. LinkedIn’s investment in video

**Verified primary evidence.** In a [LinkedIn post sharing Gyanda Sachdeva’s product announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/matthew-derella-a6483316_video-consumption-on-linkedin-growing-at-activity-7306236282077073408--oCX), Matthew Derella highlights video growth. Sachdeva describes tests of a video tab, video recommendations in the feed, and an expanded full-screen vertical player. This is a historical product announcement, not a check of which features every member has today.

LinkedIn’s [March 18, 2026 announcement](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/how-linkedin-is-helping-b2b-marketers-reach-audiences-at-scale-w) describes expanded creator sponsorships and video advertising products. It supports the claim of continued investment in video. Paid advertising and sponsorship products do not establish an organic reach advantage for an independent educator.

LinkedIn’s [March 12, 2026 feed update](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed) emphasizes professional relevance, actionable ideas, and less repetitive, low-substance content. This supports giving each clip a complete useful lesson. It does not establish that short videos receive preferential distribution.

## M02. A friend’s results after 45 videos

**User-reported anecdote, independently unverified.** Marshall reported on 2026-10-12 that a friend published 45 short videos and netted the following follower counts: LinkedIn 1,700, TikTok 1,600, Instagram 700. The account identities, starting counts, time period, topics, and use of paid promotion were not supplied. Preserve the reported counts without treating them as a forecast, a measured causal effect, or a unique audience total across platforms.

## M03. Strategy and production model

**User-selected direction and hypothesis.** In the same request, Marshall proposed hundreds of short lessons, one per topic, derived at 2–10 videos per article. His sequence is articles, short videos, conference speaking, a self-published book, a publisher pitch supported by the audience, a course assembled from the videos, and an optional podcast interviewing featured authors. The professional audience’s receptiveness and the resulting opportunities remain hypotheses to test.

The plan’s subscriber path follows the existing [posting strategy](../../../docs/2026-posting-strategy.md#identity-and-conversion-paths). Measuring response, using feedback in revision, and adding course exercises are editorial recommendations implementing the direction. No posting cadence or numerical growth target has been selected.

## Claims to avoid

Do not promise a LinkedIn algorithm boost, extrapolate the friend’s results into a follower forecast, add platform counts as unique people, or imply that an audience guarantees conference invitations or a publishing agreement.
